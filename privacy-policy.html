1. Summary

Festiva Studio makes greeting and promotional videos on your phone. The photos you pick, and the names and messages you type, are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it does not use artificial intelligence services.

2. What the app handles, and where

DataWhat happens to itLeaves your phone?
Photos you pickYou choose them with Android's photo picker, so the app can only see the photos you select. A working copy is kept in the app's private storage so your drafts keep working.No
Text you type (names, greetings, business name, phone/address, offers)Saved in the app's private storage as part of your drafts and drawn into your video.No
Videos you createSaved to your phone's Movies/Festiva folder (Android 9 and older: the app's own folder, see §7). You can delete them in the app or in your Gallery.Only if you share them

Data in the app's private storage is deleted when you delete the draft or video, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

3. Sharing your videos

When you tap Share, Android shows its share sheet, and the video goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

4. Purchases (Festiva Premium)

Premium plans are sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and checks it on your phone to unlock premium templates. Google Play's own data practices apply to purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The Google Play Billing library that the app uses to talk to Google Play may send its own technical and diagnostic data to Google. This is part of Google Play, not something we receive.

5. Analytics and crash reports

This version of the app does not send analytics or crash reports to us or to any analytics company.

*(If a later version adds optional usage statistics: they will be off unless you turn them on in Settings, will contain only which templates are used and whether exports work, and will never include your photos, names, messages or file paths. This section and the Play Store data-safety information will be updated first.)*

6. Ads

This version of the app shows no ads.

7. Permissions

  • Photos: Android's photo picker. No broad access to your gallery.
  • Internet / network state: requested by the Google Play Billing library so purchases work. The app's own code does not upload anything.
  • Billing: to buy Premium through Google Play.
  • No storage permission. On Android 10 and newer, videos are saved to Movies/Festiva; on Android 9 and older they are kept in the app's own folder (Android/data/…/Movies/Festiva), which is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Your choices

You can delete drafts and videos in the app, delete videos in your Gallery, clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Because we do not receive your photos or text, there is no copy held by us to delete. For questions, write to elixirinfotechindia@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and important changes will be shown in the app.
