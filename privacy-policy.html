1. Summary

Festiva Studio makes greeting and promotional videos on your phone. The photos you pick, and the names and messages you type, are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no sign-in, and it does not use artificial intelligence services.

3. Sharing your videos

When you tap Share, Android shows its share sheet, and the video goes to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

4. Purchases (Festiva Premium)

Premium plans are sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and checks it on your phone to unlock premium templates. Google Play's own data practices apply to purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The Google Play Billing library that the app uses to talk to Google Play may send its own technical and diagnostic data to Google. This is part of Google Play, not something we receive.

Because purchases go through the app, our Google Play listing declares purchase history as collected for app functionality (to unlock Premium). It is processed by Google Play, never shared with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Crash reports and usage statistics

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) as our service provider for two things:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — on by default. If the app crashes, a technical report helps us fix it: the error and stack trace, phone model, Android version, app version, and a random installation ID. You can turn this off at any time in Profile › Send crash reports.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) — off unless you turn it on in Profile › Share anonymous usage statistics. It records which templates are opened and whether exports succeed, with phone model, Android version and a random app-instance ID.

Neither ever includes your photos, the names or messages you type, your videos or file paths. We do not use this data for advertising and do not sell it. Google processes it under the Firebase terms and Google's privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

6. Ads

This version of the app shows no ads.

7. Permissions

  • Photos: Android's photo picker. No broad access to your gallery.
  • Internet / network state: used by Google Play Billing (purchases) and Firebase (crash reports, and usage statistics if you turn them on). Your photos, text and videos are never uploaded.
  • Billing: to buy Premium through Google Play.
  • No storage permission. On Android 10 and newer, videos are saved to Movies/Festiva; on Android 9 and older they are kept in the app's own folder (Android/data/…/Movies/Festiva), which is removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

On your phone: delete drafts and videos in Festiva Studio, delete videos in your Gallery, clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Your photos, typed text and videos are never sent to us, so there is no copy of them anywhere else.

Stop sending data: turn off Profile › Send crash reports and **Profile › Share anonymous usage statistics**.

Ask us to delete crash reports and usage statistics: e-mail elixirinfotechindia@gmail.com with the subject "Festiva Studio – delete my data". Because these reports carry only a random ID, please include your phone model and the approximate date and time of a crash, or tap **Profile › Copy diagnostics report** and paste it, so we can find them. We delete matching crash reports and analytics data in Firebase within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is kept: crash reports are deleted automatically by Firebase after 90 days even if you do not ask. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's own policy, as required for billing and tax; manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
